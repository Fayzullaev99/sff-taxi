## Review pass 2026-10-16 (autopilot, after Wave 5)

One independent read of 1e467b4..5f71f02 (money, authorization, migrations, locale). No
silent money-losing defect: promo reservation, the ledger, tips and the card paths are
serialised and idempotent; chat access is limited to the ride's current rider and driver;
migrations 0020–0029 apply cleanly on a 0019 database. Fixed on branch `autopilot`:

- **Stops changed on the trip** were priced pickup → stops → drop-off, ignoring where the car
  was: a rider could drop a far stop the driver had partly driven towards and pay less than
  the road. The change (and its preview) now routes through the car's last fix (under
  10 minutes old): pickup → reached stops → car → stops ahead → drop-off. Test in
  `ride-stops-change.test.ts`.
- **Orders for someone else** could be placed and cancelled in a loop to SMS a stranger the
  driver's phone and a live link: at most 5 per rider and 2 per passenger phone in 24 hours
  (409 with the reason). Test in `ride-for-other.test.ts`.
- **Credit lock gap**: `changeStops` now takes the rider's advisory lock before re-pricing
  the reserved discount, like an order, a release or a referral revoke.
- **Promo-code probing**: an existing but expired / inactive / used-up code now counts
  towards the 10-misses-an-hour block like an unknown one. Test in `promos.test.ts`.

Noted, not changed (product decisions for the founder):

- Referral rewards rely on `deviceId` sent by the app; a scripted client can omit it, so the
  real limits are one account per SIM, one completed ride and the monthly cap (10). Before
  advertising the programme widely: hold the referrer's reward for a few days after the
  invitee's ride, add an operator report of drivers who complete many first rides of invited
  riders, and cap compensated first rides per driver per day.
- The launch-campaign CLI re-run rewrites the GULISTON code and the referral settings with
  the campaign's values (it is documented as idempotent); run it once, or add `--force`
  semantics before operators tune those settings in the panel.
- `users.locale` is rewritten on every authenticated request whose Accept-Language differs:
  one account on two devices in different languages gets SMS in whichever spoke last.
- The partial reprice drops the discount for good when an edit makes it 0; a later edit
  raising the fare does not bring it back on that ride.
